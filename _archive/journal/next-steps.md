# Next Steps for Spiritist Inquiry

*Spiritist Inquiry: Interdisciplinary Journal of Spiritism and Contemporary Science* is an open-access, peer-reviewed journal. The next steps are editorial and technical improvements to the journal site:

1. Publish rigorously reviewed articles that engage Kardecist primary sources and the peer-reviewed scientific literature.
2. Keep English and Portuguese versions synchronized.
3. Improve navigation, accessibility, and the reading experience.
4. Provide source links and downloadable formats (PDF, HTML) for each article.
5. Maintain clear citations and a transparent double-blind peer-review process.
