The Spiritist Essays is a personal collection of essays by Ovande Furtado Jr. on Spiritism, as codified by Allan Kardec, and its dialogue with contemporary scientific research — consciousness, identity, health, moral development, and human evolution. The essays are fully cited but not peer reviewed. More about this site.

Oct 5, 2026 Continuity of Consciousness: The Perispirit, Disincarnation, and Death Anxiety in Spiritist Thought and Contemporary Science A conceptual integration essay arguing that the Spiritist model of the perispirit makes three linked claims (about the organization of the living body, the process of dying, and the psychology of death anxiety) and evaluating each against near-death experience research, bioelectricity, consciousness science, and the psychology of death attitudes.
